01 / What is ClosedEYE?

Understand the surface
before you close it.

ClosedEYE helps you understand what your computer is exposing to the network. It audits listening services, examines firewall configuration, identifies potentially risky services, and provides a safer way to reduce unnecessary inbound exposure.

The goal is not to hide your machine. It is to make its exposure visible, understandable, and easier to control.

05 / Security philosophy

ClosedEYE does not promise invisibility.

A local machine cannot determine every aspect of its Internet exposure. Router configuration, NAT, port forwarding, IPv6, VPNs, firewalls, and ISP configuration can all affect accessibility.

The goal of ClosedEYE is to reduce unnecessary exposure and make potentially risky configurations easier to understand. It focuses on practical visibility and reversible changes rather than making claims it cannot guarantee.
